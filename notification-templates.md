SST Hostel Leave Management System
Student Communication Kit
This kit holds the student and approver messages for the Hostel Leave Management System. Every student approval email carries a note making clear that hostel leave approval does not grant any academic exemption, and that academic leave or exemptions follow the Student Policy. Rejection notices, parent-rejection notices, and warden or POC Slack alerts are operational and do not carry this note. Placeholders in braces (for example {Student Name}) are filled by the system at send time.
1. Warden approval for Re-Examination (Exam Dates Differ for Batch and GP)
Leave Created
No notification required.
Policy Check (Auto-Rejection)
Mail to Student, Parent in CC.
Subject: Leave During Examination Request Rejected
Dear {Student Name},
Your Leave During Examination request for {Start Date} to {End Date} could not be approved, as the requested dates do not match the approved examination schedule under the hostel leave policy.
If you believe this is an error or need any clarification, please contact the Hostel Administration.
Regards,
Hostel Administration
Scaler School of Technology
